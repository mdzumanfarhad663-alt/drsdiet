Our story

Meet the Founder

Dr. Baldev Sandhu, M.D., plastic surgeon and founder of The Doctor's Diet.

There is one patient I remember whenever I think about how my understanding of weight loss changed.

She came to me as a plastic surgeon because she wanted to look better. There were areas of her body that bothered her, and she believed that liposuction was the way to achieve the appearance she wanted.

So I performed liposuction. I removed the fat. Her body was reshaped, and she was pleased with the result.

But sometime later, she came back. The weight had returned.

The fat I had removed was gone, but she had gained weight again. Her body had changed, and the areas that had bothered her were once again a concern.

That experience made me look at liposuction differently. Liposuction could remove unwanted fat and reshape the body, but it could not address why the patient had gained weight in the first place. It was a cosmetic procedure that could change someone's appearance, but it was not a treatment for excess weight itself.

That lesson stayed with me throughout my career as a plastic surgeon.

Over the years, I saw many patients who came to me for liposuction. They weren't necessarily looking for surgery. They simply wanted to look better. They believed that removing unwanted fat was the way to accomplish that goal, and at the time, liposuction was one of the primary tools available to me.

Then medicine changed.

The GLP‑1 Era

When GLP‑1 medications became available for weight management, my patients suddenly had another way to achieve something they had previously come to my office seeking through surgery.

Gradually, I performed less liposuction. Eventually, I stopped performing it altogether.

But my career took another unexpected turn.

As I followed patients using GLP‑1 medications, I began hearing about problems such as nausea, bloating and constipation. I also became increasingly interested in what was happening to the gastrointestinal system and in the loss of muscle that can accompany significant weight loss.

The scale was going down. But I began asking myself:

What else was going down with it?

That question became increasingly important to me.

I realized that helping someone lose weight was only part of the equation. As a physician, I wanted to understand how we could help patients maintain their nutrition, support their digestive health and preserve muscle while pursuing their weight-loss goals.

Why I Created The Doctor's Diet

My career had taken me from helping patients change the outside of their bodies through plastic surgery to thinking much more deeply about what was happening inside their bodies during weight loss.

That evolution led me to create The Doctor's Diet.

The Doctor's Diet is built around a simple idea: weight loss should be about more than losing pounds. It should also be about nutrition, digestion, muscle and overall wellness.

That philosophy is the foundation for the Doctor's Diet products, Morning Greens, Probiotics and Creatine, and for the broader approach behind the brand.

I created The Doctor's Diet because I believe patients deserve to look beyond the number on the scale and understand what is happening to their bodies along the way.

— Dr. Baldev Sandhu
