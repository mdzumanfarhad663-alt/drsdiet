On GLP‑1s · 7 min read · By Dr. Baldev Sandhu, M.D.

The Day-to-Day Foundation

Why a simple daily structure matters while you're on a GLP‑1.

Many people see GLP‑1 medications as a standalone solution. But the medication changes how your body handles hunger. It doesn't build the habits that protect your health along the way. That part is up to you, and it's simpler than it sounds.

Why structure matters

GLP‑1 medications slow how quickly your stomach empties and help you feel full for longer. That's what makes them effective. It also means you're eating much less, which brings three things worth paying attention to.

  • Muscle. In a large calorie deficit, the body can draw on lean muscle as well as fat. Studies suggest a meaningful share of weight lost on GLP‑1s can come from lean mass. Muscle matters for strength, energy and how your body uses calories.
  • Nutrition. Smaller portions can mean fewer vitamins and minerals. Some people notice fatigue or low energy.
  • Habits. The medication quiets hunger for now. If you build healthy routines while you're on it, you'll have them when appetite returns.

A daily routine helps on all three fronts: it supports your nutrition, helps protect muscle and turns good choices into habits.

The three anchors

The Doctor's Diet routine is built around three small steps, each done at the same point in your day.

1. Morning Greens

Because digestion slows on a GLP‑1, many people feel sluggish or irregular. The medication can also mute thirst, so it's easy to fall behind on fluids.

  • Hydration first. Start the day with a full glass of fluid.
  • Gentle digestive support. Greens supply plant ingredients that support regularity.*
  • A nutritional baseline. When solid food is unappealing, greens help fill micronutrient gaps.*

How: mix one scoop into 8 oz of ice-cold water within 30 minutes of waking.

2. Probiotic

When food stays in the stomach and small intestine longer, some people experience bloating and discomfort. A healthy gut microbiome supports comfortable, efficient digestion.

  • Digestive comfort. Beneficial bacteria support healthy digestion.*
  • Regularity. A daily probiotic supports regular intestinal transit.*
  • Getting more from less. A balanced gut supports nutrient absorption when portions are small.*

How: take one capsule with water 30 minutes before your first meal. Our probiotic uses MakTrek Bi-Phasic technology to help cultures survive stomach acid.

Greens when you wake, a capsule before breakfast, creatine at lunch.

3. Creatine

Creatine monohydrate is one of the most studied supplements for muscle. Paired with protein and movement, it helps your body hold on to the muscle you have.

  • Lean muscle. Creatine helps preserve lean muscle mass during weight loss.*
  • Energy. It supports the body's natural energy system, without stimulants.*
  • Strength. It supports strength and physical performance.*

How: take 3–5 g daily. Consistency matters, so blend it into a midday protein shake.

Make it automatic

None of this needs to be complicated. Do it every day and it stops being a decision.

Always talk to your doctor before starting supplements, especially while taking a prescription medication.
